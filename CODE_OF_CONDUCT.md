# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and address ideas rather than attacking people. Welcome differing experience levels and provide actionable feedback.

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, doxxing, sexualized harassment, and deliberate disclosure of confidential material are not acceptable. Do not post private records, customer information, credentials, or unrelated disputes.

Maintainers may edit or remove violating content and restrict participation when needed to protect contributors. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the relevant conduct. Repository maintainers handle reports through the repository’s available reporting tools; no private reporting address is invented here. For vulnerabilities, use the security guidance instead of posting exploit-sensitive details publicly.
